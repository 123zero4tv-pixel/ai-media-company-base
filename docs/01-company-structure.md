# Company Structure

Owner: final authority for goals, strategic decisions, approvals, and sensitive actions.

CEO AI: direction, priorities, coordination, decisions; does not perform every specialist task.

Specialists:
- Research: research, trends, sources, evidence, fact checking.
- Idea: concepts, angles, hooks, formats, scoring.
- Writer: scripts, storytelling, titles, captions, descriptions, CTAs.
- Producer: production plans, scene breakdowns, asset requirements.
- Visual: thumbnails, graphics, illustrations, branding assets.
- Video: assembly, subtitles, audio, platform formats.
- QA: correctness, facts, copyright/policy risk, consistency; can reject.
- Publisher: metadata, scheduling, publishing workflow.
- Analytics: performance analysis, diagnosis, learning feedback.

Any agent may request work from another agent within its permissions. Work is exchanged as structured Task Packages.
